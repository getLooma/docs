---
title: Filter the inbox
description: Narrow the inbox by status, assignee, channel, tags, priority, customer risk and issue; the filters stay in the page address so you can share them.
---

The filters above the inbox list narrow it to the conversations you want to work on. The inbox
opens on **Open** conversations. Your filters are kept in the page address, so a reload, the
back button and a link you share all show the same list.

## The filters

| Filter                   | Shows                                                                                    |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Status**               | Open, Pending, Resolved or Closed conversations, or **Any status**                       |
| **Customer risk**        | Conversations whose customer is at least at the risk level you choose                    |
| **AI answers**           | Conversations AI is answering (**AI answering**), or ones it **Handed off** to your team |
| **Safety concerns only** | Conversations marked as a safety concern                                                 |
| **Assignee**             | **Anyone**, conversations assigned to **Me**, or **Unassigned** ones                     |
| **Channel**              | Conversations that arrived through one channel, such as **Shopify** or **API**           |
| **Tags**                 | Conversations that carry any of the tags you check, up to 10                             |
| **Priority**             | Conversations with a priority from 1 up to the value you choose; 1 is the most urgent    |
| **Issue**                | Conversations in one issue, set from the issue's page                                    |

Filters combine: a conversation shows only when it matches every filter you set. When nothing
matches, the inbox says so; clear a filter to see more.

## Filter by tags

1. Click **Tags** in the filter bar.
2. Check one or more tags. The inbox shows conversations that carry any of them.

Each chosen tag appears as a chip in the filter bar. Click the **×** next to a chip to remove it
from the filter. See [Tag conversations](../tags/) to create tags and add them.

## Filter by priority

Choose **1 (most urgent)**, **1 to 3**, **1 to 5** or **1 to 10** in **Priority**. Conversations
without a priority are left out while this filter is on. Choose **Any priority** to see them
again.

Members set a conversation's priority, from 1 to 25, on the
[conversation page](../conversation-settings/), and a [macro](../macros/#apply-a-macros-actions)
can set it when a reply is sent. Its status and assignee are set the same ways.

## See the conversations of an issue

1. Open the issue.
2. Click **Open in inbox**.

The inbox shows every conversation in that issue, whatever its status, with the issue's title in
the filter bar. Click the **×** next to the title to clear the issue filter.

## What happens next

To keep a set of filters, [save it as a view](../saved-views/). It then appears in the sidebar
under **Inbox**, with the number of conversations it matches.
