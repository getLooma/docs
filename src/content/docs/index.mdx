These pages explain how to use Looma, from your first integration to everyday work in the inbox.
They are written for support agents and the admins who set Looma up for their team.

The documentation is open source. If something is wrong or missing, use **Edit page** at the
bottom of any page, or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/getLooma/docs).
