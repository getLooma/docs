date: 2026-11-24
title: Macro actions, Save as macro, and status and priority controls
category: new
docs: /inbox/macros/
---

A macro can now set the conversation's status and priority, assign it and add tags when the
reply is sent with **Apply actions** ticked. Admins can save a sent reply as a new macro. Any
member can now set a conversation's status, assignee and priority from 1 to 25 on the
conversation page.
