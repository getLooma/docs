---
date: 2026-11-24
title: Tag an issue's conversations automatically
category: new
docs: /inbox/tags/
---

Admins can link up to three tags to an issue from its page. Every conversation in the issue gets
them, both the ones already in it and the ones added later, and the conversation page marks them
**auto**. A tag a member removes stays removed. Settings → Tags shows how many issues each tag is
linked to.
