title: Tags, more inbox filters and saved views
category: new
docs: /inbox/saved-views/
---

Admins create up to 100 tags in Settings → Tags, and any member adds up to 10 to a conversation.
The inbox now filters by assignee, channel, tags, priority and issue, and keeps its filters in the
page address. Save filters as a view in the sidebar, with a count of matching conversations:
personal views for yourself, shared views for the whole organization.
