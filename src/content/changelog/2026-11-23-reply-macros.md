title: Reply with macros
category: new
docs: /inbox/macros/
---

Admins save the replies your team writes most as macros, with variables such as the customer's
first name or latest order number. Press Ctrl+/ (⌘/ on macOS) in the reply box to insert one.
Looma fills in the variables for the conversation, and a reply can't be sent until every missing
value is filled in.
